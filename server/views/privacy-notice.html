The Environment Agency is the data controller for the report an environmental problem service on GOV.UK.

A data controller determines how and why personal data (personal information) is processed.

Our personal information charter explains:

  • what we do with caller's personal information (personal data) in general
  • caller's rights
  • how to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office

Why we collect personal data

We collect and process personal data as necessary to exercise our official authority set out in law under:

  • the Environment Act 1990
  • Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) Regulations 2016
  • the Environment Act 1995

The lawful basis we rely on to process caller's personal data is article 6(1)(e) of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What personal data we collect

We collect the following personal data from a caller directly:

  • name
  • phone number
  • email address, if given
  • home address, if given
  • details of health issues and treatment connected to the problem reported, if given
  • any other details a caller includes about themselves

We may also ask for and collect personal data about other people if they are potentially involved in illegal activity. This could include:

  • a description of the person
  • details of their vehicle, including registration number
  • any other details a caller give

What we do with callers' personal data

We use personal data to:

  • help process reports, for example to find the location of a problem we are told about
  • respond to callers about a report, if necessary
  • improve the service by monitoring how it's used

We may share personal data with emergency services and environmental response teams outside the Environment Agency if it is essential to deal with the problem being reported.

We will not share personal data with anyone else without consent, unless we must do so by law.

We do not use personal data to make any automated decisions or for automated profiling.

Callers can remove consent for us to use their personal data at any time by contacting:

Email: enquiries@environment-agency.gov.uk
Telephone: 03708 506 506

Where personal data is stored and processed

We store and process personal data on secure servers in the United Kingdom (UK).

Data is not transferred outside the UK.

How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data indefinitely.

Changes to this notice

We may change this privacy notice. Any changes to this privacy notice will immediately apply.

If these changes affect how personal data is processed, we'll take reasonable steps to let anyone affected know.

This notice was last updated on 27 January 2025.
